# Genii survey evidence

The language used to describe a respondent's answers and the provisional portrait drawn from them.

## Language

**Response**: A person's selected answer or written reply to a particular question. A response is self-reported, not independently observed behavior.

**Context fact**: An explicitly reported circumstance, such as living alone or the close person selected for this conversation.

**Evidence observation**: A specific piece of information attributed to one response, with its question, subject and time window retained.

**Behavioral dimension**: A named aspect of behavior, such as planning or conflict style, for which responses can provide supporting or conflicting observations.

**Routine measure**: A directly reported frequency or pattern within a stated period. Its position does not represent overall health or prediction accuracy.

**Usual pattern**: The respondent's report about their past month, unless the question explicitly defines another period.

**Recent state**: The respondent's report about the last seven days, kept distinct from their usual pattern.

**Interpretation**: A provisional plain-language claim drawn from evidence, distinguishable from the respondent's literal answer.

**Evidence confidence**: The strength of support for an interpretation. The current implementation's source-count labels are a provisional approximation, not calibrated probabilities.

**Held-out check**: A question whose answer is withheld from the profile used to predict that answer.

**Endorsement**: True/False feedback on an interpretation. It expresses resonance and does not independently demonstrate prediction accuracy.

**Other response**: A reply outside the authored options; preserved without assigning an authored score.
