# Reference mechanics and research limits

Research date: 2026-09-14. These external examples inform proposals; they do not verify MirrorMii product truth or prove the proposed campaign will spread.

- [Outfit7's Talking Ben listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?gl=UK&hl=en-GB&id=com.outfit7.talkingben): the developer describes reactive character interactions and sharing recorded moments. Inference for this product: short visible reactions can make Genie memorable; quiz flow should stay easy to complete.
- [16Personalities](https://www.16personalities.com/) and [its terms](https://www.16personalities.com/terms): a free assessment/result and paid deeper material provide a concrete packaging analogue. They do not establish demand or pricing for our product.
- [16Personalities framework reference](https://www.16personalities.com/for-ai): its model is distinct from MBTI. Treat both as reference products; choose an original promise, taxonomy, questions, and scoring.
- [Lark plan comparison](https://www.larksuite.com/en_us/plans): published rows/table differ across plans; current displayed limits are 2,000 on Starter/Basic, 20,000 on Pro, and 50,000 on Enterprise. These are vendor plan descriptions, not a verification of MirrorMii's tenant. Endpoint throughput and concurrency remain unverified. Browser access to the Lark developer documentation was denied and that route was stopped.
- [Kun's task workflow](https://github.com/kunchenguid/kun/blob/main/ENTRY.md) informed research and iterative visual planning. [Lavish Editor](https://github.com/kunchenguid/lavish-axi) supplies a local review option. Neither establishes a product strategy specific to this quiz; the product proposals are our analysis of the founder's brief.

Next evidence: current company facts, actual first-user interviews, result comprehension, observed friend referrals, willingness to pay if relevant, and tenant-specific Lark behavior with synthetic data. Do not substitute a competitor's popularity for this product's validation.
