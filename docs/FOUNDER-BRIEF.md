# Genie Personality Survey Campaign — Full Brief

## Core Concept
This campaign is a funny conversation with an AI that gets increasingly good at reading the user—not a wellness assessment.

> **Answer suspiciously random questions. Genie will try to figure out who you are.**

Front end: group-chat humor, absurd hypotheticals, gossip, playful accusations. Back end: behavioral inference.

**Key principle:** users should not know exactly what Genie is measuring. Important latent traits are tested multiple times through different disguises. Contradictions are useful data.

## Three Survey Depths

### Level 1 — Can Genie Clock You?
**10–12 questions / ~2 minutes.** Viral top-of-funnel. Promise: “Let me make a suspiciously specific read of you.”

Example questions:
- Plans get cancelled 30 minutes before you leave. First emotion? THANK GOD / actually excited / find someone else / already removed my pants / depends who cancelled.
- You have $300 of irresponsible money. Where does it disappear? Food / appearance / experience / hobby obsession / someone I love / save it.
- Pick your most toxic relationship: alarm / phone / bed / DoorDash / calendar / unnamed human.
- You are exhausted and someone asks “Are you okay?” I'M FINE / life story / just tired / joke / cry / depends who asks.
- Your friend is making a terrible decision: stop them / warn once / support the delusion / ask questions until they realize it / popcorn.

Midway, Genie makes an accusation:
> **EARLY ACCUSATION:** I think you're easier on other people than you are on yourself.

Responses: **Nope / Maybe / That's rude because it's true.**

If rejected:
> **Noted. My case against you has weakened. Continue.**

Ending:
> ## YOU SUMMONED
> # THE FUNCTIONAL MENACE
> *Somehow still employed.*
>
> You appear organized because you are extremely good at solving problems after you've created them.
>
> **Genie confidence: 43%**

Conversion: **I know enough to judge you. Not enough to impersonate you. Get me to 70% →**

### Level 2 — How Well Can Genie Read You?
**25–35 questions / ~7 minutes.** Promise: “Give me seven minutes. I'll tell you things you didn't tell me.”

Re-test Level 1 dimensions through completely different disguises.

Examples:
- Unexpectedly empty Saturday: which outcome feels worst—accomplished nothing / saw nobody / spent too much / never left house / someone ruined peaceful day?
- Permanently delete one inconvenience: deciding food / clothes / workouts / replying / appointments / groceries / remembering obligations.
- Wake up feeling awful but calendar has plans: body wins / calendar wins / depends who / coffee gets custody / renegotiate every 14 minutes.

Personal Operating Manual:
- When energized: spontaneous + ambitious
- When depleted: avoidant + comfort-seeking
- Decision path: people → intuition → evidence
- Protects: other people's expectations
- Sacrifices first: own recovery
- Hates being told what they “should” do
- Best influence style: options, not orders

> **Something you didn't tell me directly:** You don't actually dislike routines. **You dislike routines you didn't choose.**

### Level 3 — Build My Genie
**~80–120 structured prompts**, broken into short sessions over several days. Promise: “Teach an AI enough about you that it can start predicting your choices.”

Never market this as a 100-question survey.

> **Think Genie knows you? Let's find out if I can become you.**

User-facing chapters:
- **Your Crimes**
- **People You'd Hide a Body For**
- **Your Questionable Financial Decisions**
- **Things Tomorrow You Would Like to Discuss**
- **Your Body Has Entered the Chat**
- **Chaos Department**
- **Who Put You in Charge?**
- **Future You Has Notes**

Use **counterfactual twins**: same hidden dilemma, changed context. Example: exhausted + best friend dinner / boss dinner / new date dinner. This learns contextual preference structures rather than simplistic universal rules.

## Psychological Masking Strategy
Design around disguises, not survey categories:
1. Absurd hypotheticals
2. Pick your crime
3. Judging-a-friend / gossip
4. Money under artificial constraints
5. Private-self vs public-self
6. Social micro-conflicts
7. Future-You consequences
8. Genie accusations/predictions

Hidden constructs can include autonomy/reactance, conscientiousness, reward sensitivity, novelty seeking, social accommodation, people-pleasing, uncertainty tolerance, body awareness, planning horizon, impulsivity, recovery style, social energy, communication preference, self-presentation, trust hierarchy, evidence preference, price sensitivity, routine dependence, eating/sleep context, stress behavior, delegation preference, support preference, and conflict style.

**Rule:** never make the healthy, responsible, or socially desirable answer obvious.

## High-Value Question Patterns

**Your friend says “I don't care where we eat, you pick.” What happens?**
Pick / offer three / research six menus / anything-is-fine deadlock / already know what they want.

**Someone gives you a birthday gift you hate. Your face does what?**
Academy Award / fake it four seconds / make giver feel great / ask for receipt / friends know better.

**You're freezing. Everyone says the room is fine.**
Say it / quietly find jacket / democracy has spoken / change thermostat / leave.

Together these triangulate accommodation, conflict avoidance, autonomy, adaptation, bodily awareness, and willingness to inconvenience others.

**You're home alone. What behavior immediately becomes legal?**
Eat over sink / ancient clothes / garbage TV / talk to yourself like multiple employees / order food despite having food / behave exactly the same.

**How many people could FaceTime you right now without you checking your face first?**
Zero / one / 2–3 / inner circle / anyone.

**You get $500 nobody knows about and it must disappear by midnight.**
Clothes/beauty / expensive food / boring needed item / someone else / save it despite rules / experience / exact item already known.

**Which crime against Future You are you most likely to commit tonight?**
One more episode / revenge bedtime procrastination / financially inexplicable purchase / wash hair tomorrow / text forbidden person / open fridge seven times.

**Friend complains about the same situationship for six months.**
Leave him / support women's rights and wrongs / show messages / participating in own documentary / secretly tired.

**Horrific day: pick your emotional support animal.**
Bed / fridge / credit card / group chat / gym / Netflix / “I'm fine”—extremely suspicious animal.

## Prediction + Confidence
Genie should interrupt:
> **Okay. I'm going to make an accusation.** You are more likely to cancel something because you're tired than because you're busy.

Responses: **Wrong / Annoyingly close / How the fuck did you know?**

Eventually:
> **I think you're going to choose B. Was I right?**

**Confidence = progression mechanic. Genie = identity mechanic.**

Confidence should reflect repeated evidence, consistency, contradictions, prediction performance, and uncertainty—not simply question count.

## Genie Personality System
Desired reaction:
> **“OMG. That's my Genie.”**

The result is a **character summoning/reveal system**. Use roughly 6–8 core Genie families plus secondary traits/modifiers rather than dozens of mutually exclusive boxes.

Example:
> **THE FUNCTIONAL MENACE — Chaos Genie, with Caretaker tendencies and a suspicious amount of Analyst energy.**

### 🔥 Chaos Genie
- **The Functional Menace** — *Somehow still employed.*
- **The Delulu Strategist** — *Bad idea. Excellent execution.*
- **The Side Quest CEO** — *Started one task. Founded three companies.*

### 👑 Commander Genie

- **The Human Override Button** — *Recommendations are welcome. Instructions are not.*

### 🛋️ Comfort Genie

## Mildly Offensive-but-Accurate Lines
Every Genie gets one loving roast: sharp enough to trigger “fuck, that's me,” but not genuinely insulting.

- **Caretaker:** You say “I don't mind” so often that Genie has determined you occasionally do, in fact, mind.
- **Analyst:** You don't procrastinate. You conduct unauthorized additional research.
- **Chaos:** Your calendar is less of a schedule and more of a collection of allegations.
- **Comfort:** You aren't unmotivated. The task simply failed your cost-benefit analysis.
- **Commander:** You love advice right up until the moment it becomes an instruction.
- **Main Character:** Approximately 14% of your decisions are made for future lore.
- **Stability:** Your idea of spontaneity is changing the reservation from 7:00 to 7:30.

This loving roast may be more important to virality than the formal explanation because it creates the screenshot moment: **“Fuck. That's me.”**

## Social / Viral Loop
The goal is not merely “share your result.” Make comparison part of the game:
- “NO WAY you're a Caretaker Genie.”
- “I knew you were a Functional Menace.”
- “Send me yours.”
- “Think Genie got you wrong? Send me to someone who knows you.”

The personality system should invite disagreement. Disagreement creates conversation, retesting, and sharing.

## Asset Mapping
Do **not** finalize the taxonomy in isolation from existing Genie artwork. First map each existing Genie asset by visible personality cues—chaotic, regal, analytical, cozy, nurturing, expressive, resilient, etc.—then align behavioral family, naming, animation, copy, and result tone to the asset.

The character and psychological archetype should feel inseparable.

## Product / Research Architecture
Keep the three datasets nested:

**Quick variables ⊂ Medium variables ⊂ Deep variables.**

This enables measurement of how much additional questioning actually improves prediction/confidence rather than merely collecting more data.

Each important latent variable should have multiple observations through different masks. Store provenance, uncertainty, contradictions, and context. Do not promote a funny one-off answer into a stable personality claim.

## Tone Rules
- Funny first; psychological depth underneath.
- Meme-level naming, but avoid slang so temporary that it dates the product immediately.
- Mildly exposing is good; humiliating is not.
- Avoid clinical diagnosis and deterministic personality claims.
- Avoid moralizing answers.
- Do not call Comfort users lazy.
- Let Genie be cheeky, observant, and confident while preserving uncertainty.
- A wrong prediction should make the game better, not feel like system failure.
- Results must feel specific enough to screenshot but grounded enough to feel earned.

## Core Product Thesis Demonstrated by the Campaign

> **The more Genie observes your seemingly insignificant decisions, the better it becomes at understanding how you make choices.**

The viral quiz earns attention. The personality reveal creates identity. Confidence creates progression. Predictions demonstrate learning. The deeper survey begins constructing a contextual decision model that can later inform how the agent communicates, recommends, and delegates with that individual.
