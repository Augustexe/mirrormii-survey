# Synthetic segment fit and market model

Status: **planning research, not company canon and not customer data.** Every persona, photo and biometric below is invented; fit scores are model judgment. Validate against real Genii survey responses and install analytics before quoting any of it.

## SYNTH-30: which segments would love MirrorMii

Thirty synthetic users, anchored to the 22 ICPs in MirrorMii OS Base, each with a simulated photo day, an Apple Health week, the tension between the two, 4 to 6 personalised Genii questions and a 0 to 100 segment-love score.

- Average score 71.4. Tiers: Champion 80+ (8), Strong 70 to 79 (9), Winnable 60 to 69 (8), Hard under 60 (5).
- Champions: cozy and character-collection gamers, the data-plus-delight bridge (biohacker who also wants delight), the "be seen, not graded" cohort, score refugees who quit a wearable, skincare and glow, Chinese diaspora.
- Strong: more score refugees, Gen Z skincare, GLP-1 and weight loss, creators, longevity-curious, perimenopause. Each is one guilt or score mechanic away from churn.
- Hard: discipline-identity challengers, hardcore quantified-self optimisers, casual dabblers with no pain point, chronic deferrers.
- Through-line: photos show the curated self, biometrics show the neglected one. MirrorMii's edge is making the invisible visible without a punitive number.
- Risk flagged repeatedly: the live App Store listing shows a "Glow Score 64/100", which contradicts the no-score spec and is the likeliest trust breaker for score refugees and critics.

## Customer profile v0.1

**The seen-not-graded player:** a visually driven, character-loving adult, core age 20 to 40 (to the 50s for wound-healers), female-leaning (about 65 percent, model estimate), who wants to feel seen and cared for through a life they can watch flourish, not measured or nagged. Health is the material; the character is the motivation.

| Archetype | Who | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cozy Collector | Cozy and collection gamers | Highest fit and retention, best early monetisation (cosmetics) |
| Wound-Healer | Score refugees, the unseen, GLP-1, perimenopause | High loyalty, fragile trust; the no-score contract is the draw |
| Bridge | Wants data and delight together | The north star MirrorMii is built for |

Ranked jobs: see my effort without grading it; give me a character to love and grow; reflect the brighter self I am becoming; understand my life stage or culture; data and delight in one place. Anti-personas above are "not first", not "never".

Positioning implication: a cozy character game that runs on your real life and never grades you. Lead acquisition with Miia and the world, not tracking. Monetise like a cozy or gacha title, not a wellness subscription.

## TAM, SAM, SOM v0.1 (US, annual, 2025 basis)

| Layer | Users | Revenue | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| TAM | 135M | about $1.6B | Union of US smartphone adults using wellness or fitness apps (95M) and cozy or character mobile gamers (60M, assumption) minus overlap (20M, assumption), at $12 all-in ARPU (assumption) |
| SAM | 76.5M | about $0.9B | TAM weighted by the SYNTH-30 Champion plus Strong share (17 of 30, 56.7 percent) |
| SOM, year 3 | about 765K MAU | $2.3M to $9.2M | 1 percent of SAM; low end is 5 percent paying at $60, high end is $12 all-in ARPU |

Verified anchors used: US adults 262.0M (Census Vintage 2025), smartphone ownership 91 percent (Pew), about 95M monthly wellness-app users. Cross-checks: North America wellness apps about $1.7B, US fitness apps $12.1B, US cozy games about $973M, global gacha $22.5B (broad definition; a narrow source says $3.75B).

Sensitivity: ARPU $8 to $25 moves TAM from $1.1B to $3.4B; a global layer is plausibly 4 to 6 times US; SAM fit weight 40 to 70 percent moves SAM from $0.65B to $1.1B.
