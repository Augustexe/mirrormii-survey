# VOICE: how every survey card sounds

Status: APPROVED by Jerry, 2026-09-30 ("go ahead"). Once approved, this page wins on how cards sound and read. The card-writer skill (`skills/shared/genii-card-writer/SKILL.md`) keeps structure, evidence and JSON; LAUNCH-SPEC keeps the rules and numbers. When they disagree about voice, this page wins.

Every batch of cards is written against this page, then checked. Jerry reviews this page, not every batch.

---

## 1. Who we are talking to

- 20 to 35, on a phone, arriving from a TikTok or Instagram share. Women first; love and couples matter.
- They came for fun, not a test. They stay because a card makes them go "oh no, that's me."
- They picked a tone in the lobby: **Make it fun** or **Heart to heart**. Same card, same meaning, two voices (section 4).
- Our two app audiences (tracker users, cozy gamers) matter only on the last screens (why download). They never change how a card sounds.

**The job of a card:** make them feel caught in 4K, in a way they would screenshot and send to the group chat.

## 2. The lens: Rice Purity x Spotify Wrapped

- **Rice Purity:** specific, slightly embarrassing, real micro-behaviors. Not "are you open to new experiences?" but "you bought greens for a salad and watched them liquefy in the crisper."
- **Wrapped:** play their own behavior back to them so it feels like proof, not a horoscope.
- **The group-chat test:** would someone screenshot this card and send it to friends to argue about? If not, it is not done.

## 3. Two ways a card is great (pick one per card)

**A. A real moment that stings.** Something 20-somethings actually live, with a social cost: money between people, pride, being chosen or not, an awkward text, being caught.
Approved: the ex's parent texts "Nothing bad!" two years after the breakup. You slept through your best friend's moving day. You booked the only quiet room for your review and a coworker needs it for a job interview in four minutes.

**B. A funny absurd premise that reads in one go.** Impossible, pictured instantly, and underneath it a real social instinct.
Approved: every winter one sibling spends a week as a pumpkin. You wake up as a goldfish for a year and your parent and your partner both come for your bowl.

**Neither is allowed to be:** plain adult admin (savings accounts, apartments, tech support, logistics), a values poll, a coaching topic dressed as a scene, or an absurd premise that needs a second read.

**One premise, one beat.** If it needs "and then" twice, it is two cards. Under about 30 words.

## 4. The two voices (the two "skills")

Write the card once as a set of moves. Then render it in both voices. Meaning, option order and evidence are identical; only the sound changes.

### 4A. Make it fun (the default)

Sounds like the funniest person in your group chat, age 24 to 30, texting. Internet-native, never a brand doing Gen Z.

- Contractions always. Fragments welcome. Short.
- A concrete comic detail in the premise and in every answer: "It smells like fries." "The groveling starts at minute two." "The stairwell has great acoustics."
- At most one slang term per card, and only words a 30-year-old also uses: group chat, left on read, screenshot, soft launch, the ick, situationship, Irish exit, main character, the usual. Never: slay, it's giving, bestie (in the narrator's voice), yeet, on fleek, rizz, anything that needs a footnote.
- Roast the move, never the person. Petty is fine; cruel is not.
- Quoted lines in answers are great, one per card at most.
- No emojis in card text (they are fine inside a quoted text message in a reply thread).

### 4B. Heart to heart

Sounds like your closest friend at 1am: honest, warm, a little funny, never a form.

- **Contractions always.** "It's their turn. I'm not swapping." Never "It is their turn, and I do not swap."
- Keep the true detail from the fun version (the dresser, the fries car, the microwave). Drop the punchline, not the picture.
- Name the feeling through the moment, not with therapy words. Banned: boundaries, valid, healing, trauma, toxic, energy, self-care, grading words (kind, brave, healthy, mature, selfish, responsible).
- No exclamation marks in narration (fine inside a quoted text). No caps for effect.
- Answers still sound like a person talking: "I call before I can overthink it." not "I call right away, before I can think about it."

Same card, both voices:

> **Fun:** Moving day. You slept through it. Your best friend carried a dresser down five floors with a stranger. Rank your apology:
> An 11-minute voice note. The groveling starts at minute two / Their whole kitchen unpacked while they're at work. No note / A handwritten card. One full page, zero jokes / Me at their door every Saturday this month, drill in hand
>
> **Heart:** You slept through your best friend's moving day. They carried a dresser down five floors with a stranger. How do you make it up to them? Rank these:
> A long voice note. I'm sorry for most of it / I unpack their whole kitchen while they're at work, no note / A handwritten card, a full page, no jokes / I show up every Saturday this month with a drill

## 5. Answers: four different moves, never four strengths

This is where most cards go wrong.

- **Each answer is a different move**, a thing you'd picture someone doing: call, stall, forward it to the group chat, pretend you didn't see it. Never the same move at four intensities ("do it", "do it a bit", "don't really", "never").
- **Every answer has a bite**: a detail, a cost or a joke. A sensible answer with no joke is a bland answer.
- **No responsible-adult default.** If most people would tap one answer because it sounds mature, give that answer a cost or cut it.
- **No mush, no escape hatch.** No "sometimes", "depends", "both", "talk it through". Skip and "Not my life" already exist on every card.
- 3 to 4 answers for most formats, 12 words or fewer each. Two-answer cards (this or that) only when both sides are sharp and legible.
- Answers never all open with the same word, and never repeat a line from another card.

**Enforced:** `check-bank.mjs` fails any scored card where two answers open with the same word or share a core move (split, pay, go, keep, yes, no, take, stay, send, text, call, wait, say, tell, ask, sign and more). Receipts lists are exempt.

**Scoring note (why this does not break anything):** each answer still lands on one side of the card's sub-question. Two different moves can land on the same side through different traits. Balance comes from the card as a whole (about two answers per side), not from a +2 / +1 / -1 / -2 ladder.

## 7. Format notes (voice only; structure lives in the skill)

| Format | Voice rule |
|---|---|
| Scenario | Hook plus event in one breath; the four moves carry the jokes |
| Real ("the last time...") | A specific recent moment; answers are things people actually did, told with a detail |
| Receipts | The Rice Purity format: 4 or 5 specific, slightly embarrassing things that happened lately, plus "None of these". Never private data |
| Bet (Genii's bet) | A cocky, specific guess ("I bet your emergency fund has paid for concert tickets"); answers say what really happened |
| Reply | Always show the incoming message in the thread; answers are replies you'd actually send, in your own texting voice |
| Others | Someone else did something messy; answers are your honest first reaction, not a moral grade |
| This or that | Two sharp, legible sides, each with a detail; if you can't make both sides funny, use another format |
| Role, eyes, pick two | Short vivid lines people would claim or deny with a laugh |
| Sealed | Same voice; a fresh moment Genii bets on |

## 8. Bans (short list; full list in LAUNCH-SPEC section 2 and the skill)

No em dashes. No genie, lamp or wish (Genii is a slime who guesses and bets). Say "your partner", never "your person". No gendered words. No health, body, diet, politics, religion, orientation or explicit content. Never ask anyone to open an app, account or private data. No gotcha lines, no sitcom or announcer lines. Never name the trait being measured. Brand never-say list applies (streaks, gacha, lottery, jackpot, predicts, clinically, diagnose, treat, cure, prevent).

## 9. Before a card ships (five questions)

1. Can you picture it in one read?
2. Are the answers four different moves, each with a bite?
3. Is there an answer everyone would tap to look good? Fix it.
4. Would the group chat screenshot it?
5. Do both voices mean the same thing and sound like people, with contractions?
