# Evidence acquisition shortlist

Checked 2026-09-17 local time. This is a bounded acquisition checklist, not a systematic review. These sources motivate questions and study design; none validates the Genii quiz, its profiles, or a health benefit. Proposed product implications below are our inferences. No private survey responses were acquired.

## 1. Belonging depends on context and actual opportunity

[Walton et al. (2023), Where and with whom does a brief social-belonging intervention promote progress in college?](https://www.gregorywalton.com/uploads/4/9/4/4/49448111/waltonetal2023.pdf). [Publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37141344/), DOI 10.1126/science.ade4420.

- Design/population: randomized experiment with 26,911 students at 22 institutions; online intervention before college; first-year full-time completion outcome.
- Supports: the reported intervention benefit depended on whether students' groups had opportunities to belong. A contextual question about where someone feels accepted is a reasonable hypothesis to investigate.
- Does not support: treating social contact count as equivalent to belonging; diagnosing general adult loneliness from a quiz; assuming a companion can fix an environment with inadequate opportunities. The study did not directly validate our proposed contact-versus-belonging distinction.
- Acquire next: full methods, exact items, subgroup/context definitions, effect sizes and uncertainty, and the [2024 erratum](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39265033/) before quantitative reuse. Article and abstract checked; correction content not obtained.
- Search string: `"social belonging" "opportunities to belong" context intervention adult`.

## 2. Feeling understood is a relationship construct; sleep evidence is associative

[Selcuk et al. (2017), Perceived Partner Responsiveness Predicts Better Sleep Quality Through Lower Anxiety](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1948550616662128).

- Design/population: observational sample of 698 married/cohabiting adults aged 35–86; 219 additionally completed a week of actigraphy. Publisher abstract checked; full paper restricted.
- Supports: perceived responsiveness concerns feeling understood, cared for, and validated. It was associated with subjective sleep problems and objective sleep efficiency through statistical indirect associations involving anxiety/depression.
- Does not support: causal mediation, AI equivalence to a human partner, improved sleep from feeling understood by Genii, or an individual's sleep prediction from personality answers.
- Acquire next: full instrument, measurement timing, model covariates, coefficients/intervals, and replication outside romantic relationships. Keep actual access/contact, perceived responsiveness, and sleep separate in pilot measurement.
- Search string: `"perceived partner responsiveness" "sleep" "1948550616662128"`.

## 3. Daily experience requires repeated measurement

[Kane et al. (2014), Daily Self-Disclosure and Sleep in Couples](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4229132/), DOI 10.1037/hea0000077.

- Design/population: 56 days of evening relationship diaries and morning sleep diaries; 46 wives and 38 husbands from 46 cohabiting families; multilevel analyses separated daily deviations from average self-disclosure.
- Supports: above-usual disclosure was associated with better subjective sleep quality/efficiency for wives; the pattern differed for husbands. This supplies a concrete starting design for separating within-person changes from between-person differences.
- Does not support: a uniform effect across genders, causation, broad social-context generalization, objective sleep improvement, or next-day energy benefit. Sleep was self-reported; energy remains an evidence gap in this shortlist.
- Acquire next: exact diary wording, missingness/compliance, effect sizes and uncertainty, and newer replications with objective sleep plus fatigue/vitality. Predefine evening social experience, that night's sleep, and next-day energy as different time points.
- Search string: `"daily social interaction" "within-person" sleep next-day fatigue vitality diary`.

## 4. Personal endorsement is not independent assessment validation

[Forer (1949), The fallacy of personal validation; a classroom demonstration of gullibility](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18110193/). [Original article scan, third-party host](https://criticalthinkingtext.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/forer_the-fallacy-of-personal-validation_1949.pdf).

- Design/population: historical classroom demonstration. Bibliographic identity and scan availability verified; scan text was not extractable in this pass, so sample size and numeric result are deliberately not asserted.
- Supports at this stage: a primary historical acquisition target for the distinction between perceived fit and assessment validity. It is not a sufficient modern validation framework by itself.
- Does not support: saying every positive reaction is a Barnum effect, estimating our quiz's false-positive rate, or treating satisfaction/True responses as evidence of predictive accuracy.
- Acquire next: read original scan, then obtain modern controlled feedback studies. Proposed local controls: equally warm generic feedback and shuffled feedback; blinded comparison; independent held-out answers collected before revealing predictions; separately rate warmth, specificity, felt understanding, and prediction correctness. These controls are our proposed design, not a protocol claimed to be tested by Forer.
- Search string: `"Barnum effect" personality feedback randomized generic individualized validation`; additional primary lead: [computerized personality reports study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1162908813000649), publisher search extract available but direct fetch restricted.

## 5. AI companionship: distinguish assigned treatment from voluntary use

[Fang et al., How AI and Human Behaviors Shape Psychosocial Effects of Extended Chatbot Use, revised v2, 2 October 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17473v2).

- Design/population: four-week randomized experiment, n=981, over 300,000 messages; assigned interaction mode and conversation type. Preprint abstract and revision history checked.
- Supports: outcomes included loneliness, human social interaction, emotional dependence, and problematic AI use. The revised abstract reports no significant effects of assigned conditions; greater voluntary use was associated with worse outcomes. Measure potential benefit and displacement/dependence separately.
- Does not support: causal harm from longer use, proven loneliness reduction, equivalence of all modes, or benefit/safety of Genii. A nonsignificant result is not proof of no effect.
- Acquire next: revised full methods, recruitment criteria, preregistration/deviations, attrition, baseline-adjusted effect sizes/intervals, conflicts/funding, and any later peer-reviewed version. Use v2, not the earlier launch blog's mixed-benefit framing. Add a separate primary controlled benefit study if benefit claims are contemplated.
- Search string: `"AI companion" randomized loneliness human social interaction emotional dependence preregistered`.

## 6. Advertising substantiation is claim-specific

[US FTC, Health Products Compliance Guidance (December 2022)](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance).

- Design/population: official US staff guidance, not an empirical study; includes health-related apps.
- Supports: objective express and implied claims need adequate substantiation before advertising; the guidance generally expects rigorous human randomized testing for health-benefit claims. Consumer anecdotes or experience surveys do not establish a product's health effects.
- Does not support: legal clearance for particular copy, automatic applicability of every example to Genii, Canadian compliance, or a claim that an entertainment disclaimer cancels a misleading overall impression.
- Acquire next: create a claim ledger containing exact copy, likely implied meaning, product/version, intended audience, matching evidence, and allowed wording. Obtain jurisdiction-specific review before health-benefit advertising; Canadian requirements remain outside this pass.
- Search string: `site.ftc.gov "Health Products Compliance Guidance" "apps"`; Canada follow-up: `site.competition-bureau.canada.ca performance claims adequate proper test`.

## Minimum evidence packet before stronger claims

- [ ] Obtain full text and supplements; record version/corrections, population, setting, measure wording/licensing, design, timing, estimates/intervals, exclusions, and limitations.
- [ ] Keep construct plausibility, quiz measurement validity, held-out prediction accuracy, felt-understood experience, and product-caused benefit as separate evidence questions.
- [ ] Predefine generic/shuffled comparisons and independent held-out outcomes; preserve initial outputs and later feedback separately.
- [ ] For daily sleep/energy claims, use repeated prospective measures and distinguish within-person changes from differences between people.
- [ ] For companion benefit, evaluate human contact/displacement and dependence alongside enjoyment or loneliness, with a suitable comparison and follow-up.
- [ ] Do not turn this shortlist into “scientifically validated,” clinical accuracy, causal sleep/energy, or quantified health marketing claims.
