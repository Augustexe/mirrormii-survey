# Pilot voices V1

Status: **proposed examples.** Every question below is an item already in `pilot/bank.js`. The voice changes the words only; each row is the same answer in three deliveries and records the same tag level.

## Writing rules

The test for every answer: **would someone type this to a friend, word for word?**

1. **Lead with the action.** The first 2 or 3 words say what you did. She can pick without reading to the end.
2. **8 words or fewer**, 10 at most. Prompts under 20 words.
3. **The joke is a true detail, not a metaphor.** "I ordered a salad" lands because it happened. "Vibes-based economics" is a riddle.
4. **One idea per answer.** No action plus aside plus second joke.
5. **Options start differently** so they don't blur when scanned.
6. **Voices differ in attitude, not vocabulary.** Kind: plain and warm. Fun: plus a light honest detail. Call me out: plus a self-aware jab.
7. **Same meaning, same level** across voices. A voice never makes an option more or less extreme.
8. **No cool answer.** Every option gets the same amount of charm, including the "healthy" one.
9. **Roast the move, never the person.** Nothing about identity, looks or gender. Safe for teens.

## 1. `support_capacity`: Show up anyway ↔ Say so and reschedule (Friends)

**Quick take**

| | Prompt | A | B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kind | A friend needs you, and you're exhausted. A good friend… | shows up anyway. | says so and picks another night. |
| Fun | A friend needs you. You're running on empty. A good friend… | shows up. Sleep can wait. | says "not tonight" and means it. |
| Call me out | A friend needs you. You're wiped. A good friend… | shows up. Rest is overrated. | says no. You're not a hotline. |

**Scene**

- Kind: "Last time a friend needed you and you were exhausted. What did you do?"
- Fun: "Last time a friend needed you and you were running on empty. What happened?"
- Call me out: "Last time a friend needed you and you were wiped. What did you actually do?"

| Level | Kind | Fun | Call me out |
|---|---|---|---|
| A2 | Showed up for as long as they needed. | Showed up. Stayed way too late. | Stayed till 2am. Again. |
| A1 | Showed up, but kept it short. | Showed up for an hour, then left. | Gave them an hour. Checked the time a lot. |
| B1 · guilt | Answered the next day. | Replied the next morning. Felt bad. | Left them on read. Felt guilty all night. |
| B2 | Said not tonight, and offered another day. | Said "can't tonight, tomorrow?" | Said "can't tonight." Went to bed. |

**Scene**

- Fun: "Last time the bill came at dinner with friends. How'd you split it?"
- Call me out: "Last time the bill hit the table. What happened?"

| Level | Kind | Fun | Call me out |
|---|---|---|---|
| A2 | We split it evenly. | Split it evenly. Easy. | Even split. No debate. |
| A1 · resentment | Split it evenly, though I ordered less. | Split it evenly. I'd ordered a salad. | Even split. Still annoyed about my salad. |
| B2 | Whoever had more money paid more. | Whoever's doing better paid more. | The one with money paid more. As they should. |
| none · keeps it to themselves | I paid and didn't bring it up. | I just paid. Not worth the math. | Paid it all. Acted like it was nothing. |

## How this goes into the build

- Each prompt and option in `bank.js` gets a `voices` object (`kind`, `fun`, `callout`, later `lab` and `oracle`). The engine never reads it; levels stay on the option.
- New tests: every voice has the same option count and order; options are 10 words or fewer; prompts are 20 words or fewer.
- Pilot recommendation: run the tag pilot in **Make it fun** only; compare voices after the tags pass.
