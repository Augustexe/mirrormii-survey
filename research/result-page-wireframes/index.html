The job of this page

Make them feel read in 3 seconds, prove it with their own words, then hand them to a friend and to the app.

All three drafts use the same demo reading: real library lines and real answer options from persona quiz V2 (a synthetic adult run). Only the type name is new, using the rename below. Content is fixed; only the display changes.

Filled boxes are what makes someone feel understood or makes them act. Every draft keeps this order.

My pushback

Evidence means their own words, not charts.The most convincing proof is "You told Genii: 'Went, paid, and ate instant noodles for a week.'" Quotes beat bars. The visuals show where someone lands, never a number, percentage or score. That keeps us clear of "scores" and keeps the page feeling like a friend, not a lab report.
The "that's me" moment has to be above the fold.After 69 cards, people are tired. The name plus one line that stings has to land on the first screen. In the current MVP the good stuff sits under a long scroll.
The sting is the hook. Don't bury it."You'll break any rule for your people, except the one where you tell them what hurt." That line is what makes someone feel understood. Owner-only, but big.
The page has no app bridge today.The MVP ends on the friend invite only. Every draft adds an app ending. But it can only promise what the app does now. "Miia starts as your type" would be a product feature we don't have, so it needs a decision (see below).
Old newspaper is off-brand as the main page.MirrorMii is violet, airy and glassy. A broadsheet reads great on desktop and as a screenshot, but on a phone the columns collapse into a long scroll. I'd make it the "print your edition" share format, not the default.
No layout fixes a wrong read.Blind test 1 scored 0 of 6. "7 of 8" is the strongest proof on the page when it's true and the most damaging when it's 2 of 8. Each draft frames misses as "Genii is still learning you", which doubles as the app hook. Blind test 2 still decides whether any of this lands.

The name problem, and a fix

Today: two full nicknames glued with a ×. "Open-Book Golden Retriever × Slow-and-Steady Regular" is 7 words and two nouns. It reads like a lab code, you can't say it out loud, and it doesn't fit a share card.

Proposed rule: "The [life word] [relationship noun]", 4 words at most. Who you are with people stays the noun (the identity). How you run your days becomes the adjective that colors it. Same 8 × 8 = 64 types, same scoring, only the words change.

Contradictions are a feature, not a bug: "The Full-Send Homebody" is the most shareable kind of name. Chinese names are left alone for now.

My recommendation

C for the reveal, landing on B as the page they keep. Stories carries tired players through the first 60 seconds and lands the name, the sting and the share card. Its last screen opens the Constellation, which is the full, explorable reading in brand colors. The Gazette becomes a "print my edition" share image, which is where its screenshot charm pays off.

Decisions for you

  1. Name rule: adopt "The [life word] [relationship noun]" and the 16 words above, or send edits.
  2. Which draft or which mix (my pick: C into B, with A as a share export).
  3. App bridge promise: keep it to what the app does now ("Genii keeps learning you, Miia lives your days"), or should the survey reading carry into the app (Miia starts from your type)? The second is stronger but needs product work.
  4. Hand-written connecting lines: allow short lines that aren't from the library (like the Stories miss line), or keep V2's "library lines and your answers only" rule.
  5. Low guess scores: always show the guesses, or show a softer "Genii is still learning you" when it gets fewer than 4 of 8.
