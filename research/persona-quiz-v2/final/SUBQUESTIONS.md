# Sub-question map: the evidence backbone

Built 2026-09-29. Source of truth: `subquestions.json` in this folder (this page is generated from it).

**Why this exists.** Jerry, 2026-09-29: "first establish the sub-question and then the scenario". A card starts from the plain question it answers, then hides it inside a situation people are rarely in. The sub-question is what makes an absurd scenario mean something: the river, the muffins and the jellyfish all score because a real choice sits under them.

**Counts.** 18 axis sub-questions (3 per axis), 50 tag sub-questions (2 per pair), 68 total, 204 seeds.

**How to use.** Pick a sub-question, pick a world, write the card. Absurd and unusual seeds feed scenario, reply, this or that, pick two and role. Everyday seeds feed only did formats (real = the last time, receipts = tap what's true, bet = Genii's bet). Pole A is the + pole or the A tag. Full poles, tension and risk notes live in the JSON.

**Worlds.** Absurd: impossible or magic. Unusual: possible but rare. Everyday: grounded, did evidence only.
