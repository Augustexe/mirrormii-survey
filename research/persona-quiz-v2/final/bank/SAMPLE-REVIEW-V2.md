# Sample v2: the 30 spiciest cards, judged

For Jerry. Source: `bank/sample-v2.json` (30 cards, both voices). Picked from 110 candidates (spice-A to D plus the old `sample.json`) by a panel of five synthetic players: Jasmine 27 (partnered, cozy gamer), Jordan 25 (gym trainer, not a gamer), Mika 21 (broke student, barista), Priya 33 (manager, married, close to family), Theo 31 (creator, judges virality).

**Spice score** = panel average of the 5 spice marks (stakes, no safe answer, the unsaid, self-question, group-chat test), out of 5. **New 30: 4.06. Old sample: 2.41.** Fun rose from 3.5 to 4.1; one-read clarity held (4.3 to 4.4). The lowest new card (C4-53, 3.6) still beats 28 of the 30 old cards.

**Coverage:** 7 of 7 chapters (3 to 6 each), 10 of 13 formats (no role, eyes or pick two: every candidate in those formats scored lower than what it would replace), all 6 axes on 3 or more cards (R3 and L1 exactly 3), two this-or-that rounds (`ch3-r40`, `ch5-r50`), one feeling card (C3-61 after C3-60), 3 sealed guesses, 0 timed hooks, no two-word opener used more than twice. Checker: 0 errors, 8 warnings (Heart to heart lines over 12 words on C3-60, C3-63, S-61, all from the source cards).

**Light edits (evidence unchanged, Heart to heart updated to match):** C3-60 option 1 "Wake my person. Hand them the phone. Nothing hidden." became "Wake my person. Show them everything. Nobody sleeps now." (it read as the good-person pick). C3-41 "Pass. Some doors stay shut. Mine too." became "Pass. And they don't get to read mine either." (same privacy-both-ways, less saintly). C2-71 "Grim. Somebody should tell Theo." became "Grim. I'm telling Theo. Tonight." (now it costs something). C7-71 stem "Be honest:" became "Calling it:" and C4-52 "Rank it:" became "Which would sting most?" so no stem appears twice in the same shape. S-61 gained `chapter: 3` (it had none).
