# Simulation report (persona quiz v2)

Generated by `node sim.mjs` on the assembled kit (`cards.json`: 138 chapter cards, 24 sealed cards in the pool, 8 drawn per respondent). Deterministic seed, so the numbers repeat exactly.

## Population

- **Random clickers** (400): every card answered uniformly at random, normal timing.
- **Consistent respondents** (640): a hidden position on each of the 6 axes and 25 tag pairs (uniform -1 to +1). Each card picks the option that best fits the hidden profile (small tie noise); 20% of picks are uniformly random. Feeling cards are random.
- **Skippers** (160, the 20% of the consistent population): consistent, but 25% of cards get Skip, Not my life or No recent example.
- **Speed-tappers** (120): consistent for the first half; the second half is tapped in 0.3 to 1.4 s with 60% random picks.
- Everyone plays the same bank (no age band since Build C). Each respondent's 8 sealed cards are drawn from the sealed pool (`drawFinale`); sealed answers come from the same hidden profile and the same 20% noise.

60 of 87 combinations meet every target; 87 meet every target except "all tags reachable" (coverage depends on the cards more than on the thresholds). Why the shipped values:

- **tagFire 2.25**: the highest fire threshold that keeps consistent respondents in 3 to 5 tags. Lower values (1.5 to 2.0) also pass but show random clickers 3.4 to 4.7 tags; higher values (2.75 and up) push consistent respondents under 3 and leave some with none. Support comes in steps (0.45, 0.55 or 0.80 times strength 1 to 3), so the grid moves in jumps.
- **tagStrong 3**: random clickers average well under 1.5 strong tags (the brief's bar) while consistent respondents average about 3.
- **tagFloor 1.25**: if nothing fires, the single best candidate that meets the same card rules shows as "leaning", so nobody leaves with 0 tags (it rescued the only consistent respondent who had none at tagFire 2.25).
- **flexBand 0.12**: the middle value. Wider bands raise Genii's sealed passes without raising exact hits much; narrower ones call more near-ties.

- Random clickers still see some tags (shown 4.36 on average) because their picks sometimes line up by chance; only 2.10 of them are strong. The result page labels a tag "strong" only above tagStrong.
- Recovery is measured against a synthetic hidden profile. It shows the parser is consistent with the card evidence, not that the cards measure a real person. Blind test 2 is the real check.
