# Lessons from blind test 1 (Jerry, 2026-09-26)

Kit v1 (45 cards, topic-scored), run by a Claude Code agent through lavish-axi. Raw answers are kept out of git (respondent data); this is the summary.

## Results
- Sealed checks: 0 of 6 exact (chance 25%), 2 of 6 right side (chance 50%).
- Prediction lines: 9 of 14 "That's me", 3 "Kind of", 2 "Not me". Overall 2 of 5.
- Most-you line: "In five years, you want to hear you built something people know about." Least-you: "Your family frowns at a plan. The plan shrinks before the argument grows."

## Jerry's comments, verbatim
- On the $200 jacket, the rental bill and the headphones cards: "multiple of the same answer"
- On the equal-vs-proportional hot take: "ive answered a similar question efore already"
- On the exhausted-friend hot take: "answered the same type of questions"
- Overall: "Okay half of these questions are of the same repeat questions boring af, doesnt have different sections and parsing just diabolical"
- After the run: "really bad questions, really bad answers, repeated answers, like three of the same answers"

## Causes
1. Ladder answers: four intensities of one action read as the same answer ("The $300 ones" / "The $300 ones, after a week").
2. Topic repetition: each topic appeared as scenario, hot take, real moment and sealed check; Jerry noticed by card 8.
3. No sections or variety of formats; fatigue set in and 19 of the last 23 cards were answered in under 1 second, including all 10 real-moment cards (the heaviest evidence).
4. Scoring trusted rushed taps: "strong" from two sub-second answers; the pause flag broke because the median collapsed.
5. The result was built from topic scores, not personas; lines landed one by one (64%) but the whole did not feel like him (2 of 5).
