# How the personality prediction reads

A friend who has watched you for years, gently reading you to your face. Not a report, not a diagnosis, no section labels, no trait jargon.

## Shape (120 to 180 words)

1. **A title** that works like a zodiac sign: a short name people would accept if the text is right ("The Friend Who Always Picks Up").
2. **Three short paragraphs** built from the strongest tags, the emotions that kept showing up, and one believe-versus-did split. Weave in one or two of their exact answers as details (the salad, the notes app).
3. **Genii's calls:** three arrow lines predicting what they'd do in specific situations, written as confident one-liners. Use the sealed checks and the strongest tags.
4. **What people miss about you:** one line, the tender part.

## Example (from a synthetic respondent)

> ### The Friend Who Always Picks Up
>
> You answer the 11:48pm text. Every time. Then you lie awake wondering if anyone would do the same for you.
>
> You split the bill evenly and quietly mourn your salad. You'd rather cover it than be the one who asks. That thing has been in your cart for a week, because wanting stuff out loud feels like a lot.
>
> Your people get all of you: the long talks, the grocery runs, the whole Sunday. You think everyone deserves to say "not tonight." You just haven't tried it on yourself yet.
>
> **Genii's calls**
> → Someone needs help moving Saturday. You say yes before checking your calendar.
> → A surprise $200 shows up. Half of it becomes someone else's birthday gift.
> → Your friend's new song is bad. You'll say "catchy!" and mean the chorus.
>
> **What people miss about you:** quiet doesn't mean fine. Ask twice.

## Rules

- Every sentence must trace to profile.json: a tag with strength strong, leaning or split, an emotion, or a quoted answer. "Early signal" tags may appear once, softly. Tags marked depends or unknown do not appear.
- The split is the plot twist. If there is no split, use the strongest tag's most specific answer instead.
- Never use the tag labels themselves as words ("Own orbit", "Fair means proportional"). Say what the person does.
- Never read a circumstance (a bill, money pressure) as personality.
- No diagnoses, no disorders, no attachment styles, no moral grades (kind, selfish, healthy, toxic).
- Gender neutral. Second person ("you").
