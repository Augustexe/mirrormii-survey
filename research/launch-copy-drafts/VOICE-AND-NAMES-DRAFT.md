# Second voice and English-native names (draft)

Only the wording changes here. Every option keeps its meaning, level and position, so no axis or tag value moves. The PILOT-VOICES rules still apply: options are 10 words or fewer and use no grading words.

## 1. The second voice

| Candidate | Who it's for | 3 writing rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Heart to heart** | People in a tender or busy season, and anyone who feels brushed off by jokes. They want to be understood, not entertained | 1. Keep the true detail from Make it fun and drop only the punchline. 2. Calm, full sentences, with no exclamations and no caps for effect. 3. Every option gets equal dignity, and no word like kind, brave or healthy tilts a pole |
| **Keep it simple** | Skim readers, second-language English readers and people allergic to quiz fluff | 1. A plain first-person action of 8 words or fewer. 2. No idioms, slang or jokes. 3. One idea per line, and a detail stays only if it carries meaning |
| **Like my best friend** | People who connect through closeness and group-chat energy | 1. Texting cadence: lowercase and short bursts. 2. Warmth through knowing you, and any tease lands on the situation, never the player. 3. The friend is on your side in every option |

### Recommendation: Heart to heart

- **The two voices differ in attitude.** Make it fun is playful and Heart to heart is sincere, so the onboarding pick means something (PILOT-VOICES rule 6). Like my best friend is too close to Make it fun, and teasing drifts toward roast: Call me out had the lowest honesty score (4.32), and the teen judge flagged its jabs.
- **It fits Jerry's principle.** It meets people where they are and reaches their feelings. It also carries heavy cards like caregiving (C6-4) without a voice exemption.
- **It is where "I didn't know that about me" lands.** Result lines read as insight, not as a bit.
- **Risk:** Be kind scored low on share (1.89) and leaked grading words. Heart to heart keeps Make it fun's true details and bans grading words. Share cards carry only names and hearts, which are the same in every voice.
- **Keep it simple** works better as an accessibility setting later than as a personality voice.
- **Onboarding tap (proposal):** "How should Genii talk to you?" with **Make it fun** or **Heart to heart**. The lobby's "gently / playful" reaction tap overlaps with it. Merging the two taps is your call, and neither is dropped here.
